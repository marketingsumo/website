How a fractional engagement starts

Start with the real problem, not a list of deliverables.

Audit

We look at your market, message, funnel and current marketing. You get my honest read, whether we work together or not.

Focus

We decide what needs fixing first: the story, the audience, the campaign plan, the content, or the handover between marketing and sales.

Measure

We review what matters: conversations started, sales feedback and pipeline created. We keep what works and cut what does not.
