**Short answer:** in Singapore, a fractional CMO typically costs **S$5,000 to S$8,000 a month for about one day a week**, **S$8,000 to S$15,000 for two days a week** and **S$15,000 to S$25,000 for three days or more**, according to published local pricing guides. A full-time CMO costs **S$250,000 to S$450,000 a year** in base salary, before CPF, bonus and benefits.

Those ranges are wide for a reason. Here's what moves the price, what you're comparing it against, and what's usually extra.

## What does a fractional CMO cost per month in Singapore?

The most detailed local breakdown I've found comes from [MarketingAgency.sg](https://marketingagency.sg/fractional-cmo-guide/), updated in October 2026:

| Engagement | Time | Typical monthly cost |
|---|---|---|
| Light | About 1 day a week | S$5,000 to S$8,000 |
| Standard | About 2 days a week | S$8,000 to S$15,000 |
| Intensive | 3 or more days a week | S$15,000 to S$25,000 |

Two days a week is the most common arrangement, and it's the one I'd start with. One day a week is enough to set direction but rarely enough to change much. Three or more days and you're close to paying for a full-time hire without getting one.

## What makes a fractional CMO more or less expensive?

**Time.** The number of days a week is the biggest driver, as the table shows.

**Strategy only, or strategy plus execution.** Some fractional CMOs set the plan and manage your team or agency. Others write, create and launch the work themselves. If yours does the work, you may need less agency support, so compare the total cost of getting the work done rather than the retainer alone.

**Sector expertise.** A specialist who already knows your buyers costs more per day than a generalist, and usually needs far fewer days to get going. You aren't paying for them to learn your market.

**Network and audience.** Some fractional CMOs bring relationships with the people you're trying to reach. In HR tech, an introduction to a CHRO through someone they know and trust is worth more than months of cold outreach.

## How does that compare with a full-time CMO?

Full-time CMOs in Singapore are expensive, and the salary is only part of it. [MarketingAgency.sg's 2026 salary data](https://marketingagency.sg/cmo-salary-singapore/) puts base salaries at:

| Company type | Annual base salary |
|---|---|
| Startup (Series B and later) | S$180,000 to S$264,000 |
| Growth-stage company | S$216,000 to S$300,000 |
| Mid-size MNC or large local company | S$264,000 to S$360,000 |
| Large MNC regional HQ | S$336,000 to S$480,000 |

On top of base salary you pay employer CPF, bonuses, benefits and often equity. Then there's the cost of hiring. Senior searches take months, recruiters charge a fee on the hire, and if the person isn't right, you find out after six months of salary.

A fractional CMO at two days a week runs roughly S$96,000 to S$180,000 a year at the market rates above, with no bonus, no equity and one month's notice if it doesn't work.

That doesn't make fractional the right answer for everyone. If you need someone running a team of ten every day, hire full-time. But for a growth-stage company that needs senior judgement more than full-time presence, the maths usually favours fractional.

## How does that compare with a marketing agency?

Agencies price by scope, so the comparison depends on what you're buying. A [Singapore LinkedIn agency pricing guide](https://freelanceseo.sg/blog/linkedin-marketing-agency-singapore/) gives a useful picture of B2B rates:

- **One channel**, such as company page management: about S$2,500 to S$5,500 a month.
- **Paid ads management:** about S$3,000 to S$12,000 a month, before ad spend.
- **Integrated B2B content programmes:** from about S$8,000 a month to S$25,000 or more.

The difference is what you're paying for. An agency sells execution, often delivered by account managers and junior staff. A fractional CMO sells the decisions about what to execute, and some also do the work. Many companies end up with both: a fractional CMO setting direction and an agency handling volume.

## What isn't included in a fractional CMO's fee?

Budget for these on top of the retainer:

- **Ad spend.** Paid media goes straight to LinkedIn, Google or Meta.
- **Tools and software.** Marketing automation, CRM seats, design tools.
- **Production.** Video crews, designers, printing, event costs.

How much depends on the plan. For the HR tech companies I work with, it's usually somewhere between S$1,000 and S$5,000 a month.

## How do you know if it's worth it?

Measure a fractional CMO the way you'd measure a sales hire: by what happens to the pipeline. Agree up front on what you'll look at, such as conversations started, meetings with target accounts, feedback from sales and pipeline created. Review it monthly.
