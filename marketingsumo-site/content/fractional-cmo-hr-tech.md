## What does a fractional CMO for HR tech actually do?

I set your marketing direction and do the work, so there's no handover to an account team and no junior learning your market on your budget. In practice, the work falls into four areas:

- **Get clear on the market.** Positioning, messaging and a practical go-to-market plan, built around how CHROs and HR leaders in Asia actually evaluate software.
- **Give sales something useful to work with.** Campaigns, content and sales-supporting assets designed to start relevant conversations with HR buyers, not just fill a marketing calendar.
- **Build authority in the HR market.** Whitepapers, articles, video and podcast-led content that helps your company sound like it belongs in the room.
- **Reach an audience already interested in HR and work.** When it makes sense, your message goes out through the LinkedIn, newsletter, podcast and event channels I've spent 12+ years building.

## How does an engagement work?

Every engagement starts with the real problem, not a list of deliverables.

1. **Audit.** We look at your market, message, funnel and current marketing. You get my honest read, whether we work together or not.
2. **Focus.** We decide what needs fixing first: the story, the audience, the campaign plan, the content, or the handover between marketing and sales.
3. **Execute.** I build the work with you. The person who sets the strategy also writes, creates, directs and launches it.
4. **Measure.** We review what matters to your sales team: conversations started, sales feedback and pipeline created. We keep what works and cut what doesn't.

For a VP Sales or revenue lead, that means marketing you can hold to the same standard as your own team: messaging your reps can use with HR buyers, campaigns aimed at the accounts you're trying to open, and a regular review of what's turning into conversations and pipeline.

## How much does a fractional CMO cost?

**S$7,500 a month** for about two days a week. It runs month to month with no minimum term, and one month's notice to end it.

**Included:** positioning, strategy and hands-on execution; content, campaigns and sales support; direct access to me.

**Not included:** ad spend; paid tools and software licences; external production such as video crews, designers, printing or event costs; and placements with my audience, which are [priced separately](/#pricing).

## Is this the right fit?

**It's a strong fit if:**

- you sell HR technology in Singapore or across Asia;
- you already have a product and a sales motion;
- your CEO, founder or sales leader is still carrying too much of the marketing burden;
- you need senior marketing judgement *and* hands-on execution;
- you want someone who already understands HR buyers.

**It's probably not the right fit** if you only need a cheap content factory, a social-media posting schedule or a large agency team.
