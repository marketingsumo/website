## From recruitment founder to fractional CMO

I spent more than a decade co-founding and running **RecruitPlus**, a Singapore recruitment firm I built from scratch to more than 3,000 placements and S$30M in total billings. Along the way we won HR Vendor of the Year, were shortlisted for the Global Recruiter Asia Pacific awards, and ran what was probably Singapore's first viral YouTube recruitment campaign, with 2 million views before that was even a thing. I was also named **SHRI Entrepreneur of the Year in 2013**.

Then I sold out, tried a few other things and worked for other people, and somewhere along the way realised the corporate world and I had grown apart. In 2021 I went solo. I'd failed at self-employment once before; the second time, I figured it out.

## What I do now

I'm a [fractional CMO for HR tech companies](/fractional-cmo-hr-tech/). I work with growth-stage HR tech vendors selling into Singapore and Asia, setting the marketing direction and doing the work myself: positioning, content, campaigns and sales support.

Having sat on the vendor side of the HR buying table for a decade, I know how CHROs and HR leaders evaluate software, and I've spent 12+ years building an audience among them: 49K+ LinkedIn followers in the HR and HR tech community and 18K+ subscribers to my LinkedIn newsletter.

## Books, podcasts and writing

- **Author, *No More Bosses: The Journey to Sustainable Self-Employment*** (Penguin Random House SEA, 2024). A candid guide to sustainable self-employment for mid-career professionals, including the story of my first, failed attempt. [See it on Amazon](https://www.amazon.sg/dp/9815233130).
- **Host, *Rolling Stories*.** Rolling Arrays' podcast, where I interview CHROs and HR leaders about their careers and their take on HR tech, with guests from companies such as Singtel and Central Retail. [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBWunmH4h9kfg8ZFcewiyuZpvBWJCqHD9).
- **Host, aTalent's *Trailblazer Podcast*.** Conversations with leaders from companies such as Moët Hennessy and Kredivo Group.
- **Former co-host, CNA's *Work It* podcast.**
- **Writer, *Will That Work?*** Honest, occasionally uncomfortable writing on work, careers and life beyond the hamster wheel, published since 2014 on [my blog and Substack](https://www.adriantan.com.sg/) and as a [LinkedIn newsletter](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/will-that-work%3F-6865152246192189440/).
